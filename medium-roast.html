Brew Guide

A flexible daily recipe.

Medium roast responds well to a classic balanced extraction. Use the recipes below as a dependable center point, then adjust grind before changing the dose.

Balanced Pour-over

Coffee
16g, medium grind
Water
250ml at 92–94°C
Ratio
1:15.6 coffee to water
Time
2:45–3:15 minutes

Bloom with 40ml for 35 seconds, then pour in slow circles. Grind slightly finer for more sweetness or coarser for a cleaner finish.

Flat White

Espresso
18g in, about 36–40ml out
Milk
90ml steamed milk
Suggested balance
40ml coffee + 90ml milk
Milk texture
Thin, glossy microfoam

This ratio keeps the caramel and fruit notes present. Use a cup around 150ml so the coffee does not disappear under the milk.

Recipes are starting points informed by the SCA Golden Cup brew ratio, SCA espresso research, and Nespresso's flat white recipe. Adjust grind and liquid to taste.

Dense body, roasted-cocoa depth, gentle smokiness, and a long bittersweet finish.

Best For

Milk drink lovers

Ideal for anyone who wants a powerful morning cup, enjoys traditional espresso, or prefers coffee that remains noticeable through steamed milk.

Tasting Notes

Cocoa & caramel

Expect dark chocolate, toasted caramel, roasted nuts, and a rounded finish. Excellent with dairy or oat milk.

Brew Guide

Keep it rich and controlled.

Dark roasts extract readily. Start with slightly cooler water and a shorter contact time to preserve sweetness and reduce harsh bitterness.

Strong Pour-over

Coffee
17g, medium-coarse
Water
250ml at 90–92°C
Ratio
1:14.7 coffee to water
Time

Bloom with 40ml for 30 seconds, then pour gently in two stages. Add a small splash of hot water if the finish is too intense.
